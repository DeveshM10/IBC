Our Story

A father of eighty-three,
still at his desk

Two generations, seventy-five years, and one city. This is how a family consultancy in Ahmedabad came to sit across the table from banks, government departments and the men who built C.G. Road.

75+years of combined experience
100+developers worked with
1997incorporated as a company
1981the year it began

JST Properties is a two-generation real estate consultancy in West Ahmedabad, led by Chandrakant Someshwar Joshi, eighty-three, and his son Divyam Chandrakant Joshi, fifty. Between them they hold seventy-five years and more of experience in this industry, almost all of it within fifty kilometres of where they still work.

The family business operates through Jai Shivmayam Trading Company Private Limited, incorporated on 12 September 1997, with JST Properties as its real estate consultancy brand. But the work began well before the company existed — as a sole proprietorship, in the early 1980s, with nothing behind it.

The two generations

Founder & Director · 45+ years · DIN 02020457

Chandrakant Someshwar Joshi

He started in the early 1980s with no capital, no inheritance and no contacts worth the name. What he had was a willingness to keep showing up, and a memory for what a piece of land had last changed hands for.

Over forty-five years he has watched Ahmedabad turn itself inside out — from the old business districts around the walled city, out to Ashram Road, then C.G. Road, then the long westward march to S.G. Highway, Prahladnagar, Sindhu Bhavan and the ring road. He did not read about that shift afterwards. He was in the room for a good deal of it.

At eighty-three he is still at his desk most days. That is not sentimentality on our part; clients ask for him by name.

Director · 30+ years · DIN 02020487 · GujRERA registered

Divyam Chandrakant Joshi

Thirty years in the same trade, and a deliberately different half of it. Where his father's work was built on the street and the site, Divyam's has been built on the institutional side — developers, corporates, banks and financial institutions, landowners, and the investors who buy for yield rather than to live.

That includes the part of this business most agents never see: sourcing land and T.P. plots for developers assembling a project, where the buyer is a company, the diligence runs for months, and the transaction lives or dies on title.

He is the registered real estate agent under Gujarat RERA through whom the firm operates today.

The association we are asked about most

The Sakar years

Of everyone this family has worked with over four decades, one association is the one we are still asked about.

Mr. Anil Bakeri, Chairman of the Bakeri Group of Companies, is among the most respected and genuinely pioneering figures in Ahmedabad real estate. His group, founded in 1959, created the Sakar series — the commercial buildings that gave Ashram Road and C.G. Road their spine — and in 1995 became the first developer in India to be awarded CRISIL’s highest PA-1 rating.

Chandrakant Joshi worked closely with him through a sustained run of transactions across those buildings.

  • Sakar II
  • Sakar IV
  • Sakar IX
  • Shilp — C.G. Road
  • and others besides

We mention it not as a trophy. We mention it because it is where the way this firm works was settled: you are given a mandate once, and everything afterwards depends on whether the last one went well for the other side too.

Our philosophy

“Everyone should benefit.”

A transaction where one side wins and the other loses is not a good deal. It is a deal that happens once. We would rather do business with the same people for thirty years, which means the arithmetic has to work for all of them.

  • The buyer gets the right property — at a price that still looks right in five years.
  • The seller gets the right value, and an honest number rather than a flattering one.
  • The developer gets the right opportunity, and a buyer who has already been qualified.
  • The investor builds wealth, not just a portfolio of things they own.
  • And the relationship continues after the paperwork is signed.

It is also why we will tell you not to buy something. It costs us a commission and earns us the next three.

Our promise

Never really
about the building

After seventy-five years between us, the clearest lesson is that real estate is not about concrete, land or square feet.

It is a family’s home. A business’s future. A developer’s vision. An investor’s wealth. A landowner’s legacy. Very often it is the largest single decision somebody will make with a lifetime of savings.

Which is why every assignment gets professionalism, confidentiality, transparency and the assumption that we will still be dealing with you in ten years.
