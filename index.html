08 — Questions

Asked before
every booking

What does it cost me to buy through you?

On new projects, nothing. We are an appointed channel partner, so the developer pays our brokerage out of their own marketing budget. You pay the developer’s price — and because we bring volume, that price is frequently better than the one you would be quoted walking in alone.

On resale and owner listings, brokerage is charged in the normal way and we tell you the figure before you see anything.

Why should I use a consultant instead of going direct to the builder?

Because a builder’s sales team can only recommend that builder. We hold 800+ projects from over 500 developers, so we can put three towers on the same road side by side and tell you which one has the better carpet ratio, the better common plot and the better record of finishing on time.

The comparison is the product. The booking is just the paperwork afterwards.

Which areas do you cover?

West Ahmedabad in depth — Prahladnagar, Corporate Road, S.G. Highway, Karnavati Club Road, Billionaires Road, Iscon–Ambli, Rajpath–Rangoli, Sindhu Bhavan Road, Thaltej–Hebatpur, Satellite, Vastrapur, Bodakdev, and out along the 200ft Ring Road to Shela, Shilaj, Bopal and South Bopal. We also work the Sanand corridor and roughly fifty kilometres around the city.

What is an NA plot, and why does it matter?

“NA” means non-agricultural — land whose use has been formally converted so that it can legally be built on and sold as residential plots. Agricultural land that has not been converted cannot be developed, and in Gujarat there are restrictions on who may buy it at all.

When we show you a plotting scheme we check the NA order, the title and the layout approval first. It is the most common place buyers get hurt in this segment.

Do you handle home loans and registration?

We coordinate both. Loan documentation with the lenders active on that particular project, then stamp duty, registration and handover. You will not be handed off to a stranger halfway through.

Can you help me sell or rent out what I already own?

Yes, within West Ahmedabad. Tell us the property, what you paid and when, and we will tell you honestly what it will fetch today rather than the number that would win us the mandate.

I am not in Ahmedabad. Can I still buy through you?

Regularly. We send shortlists with photographs and video walkthroughs, do the site visits on your behalf, and connect you live from the site. Many of our buyers are outside Gujarat or outside India and complete the whole purchase remotely.

If you also need a registered business address in Ahmedabad, that is our other business — IBC virtual office.
