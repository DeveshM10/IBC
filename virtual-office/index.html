09 — Legality, answered plainly

The questions your
CA will actually ask

How much does a virtual office in Ahmedabad cost?

At IBC Ahmedabad a business address is ₹6,000 per year. A registered address suitable for GST and other government registrations is ₹8,999 per year at launch pricing, rising to ₹10,999 as standard. Managed Presence, which includes visitor receptions and meeting room hours, is ₹23,999 per year.

Registration plans additionally require a one-time ₹3,000 for statutory documentation — ₹1,400 government stamp paper, ₹600 notary and ₹1,000 advocate drafting — passed on at actuals with no margin added. All prices are exclusive of 18% GST.

Is a virtual office address legal for GST registration in India?

Yes. A virtual office address is acceptable as a Principal Place of Business under the CGST Act, 2017, provided the documentation requirements are met. The governing instruction is CBIC Instruction No. 03/2025-GST dated 17 April 2025, which sets out uniform PPOB documentation rules for all premises including shared and rented spaces.

Can several companies register GST at the same address?

Yes, and this is settled. Section 25(2) of the CGST Act and Rule 11 permit it, and the Authority for Advance Ruling held in the Space Lance Office Solutions Pvt Ltd case that there is no restriction on multiple businesses sharing one registered office, each holding its own GST registration.

The conditions are that each business has its own PAN, keeps separate books, and carries on its own independent activity — all of which our agreement requires of you.

What happens if a GST officer visits the virtual office address?

We are here. That is the entire point of this business.

Our premises operate as a six-seat flexi coworking setup. Your company’s branding is placed on an available flexi seat, your name board carrying your GST number is displayed, and your file — agreement, NOC, utility bill, board resolution — is kept ready at reception. Our staff support your representative through the verification. Registrations fail physical verification when nobody is present and nothing is on the wall. Neither is true here.

Who issues the No Objection Certificate for the address?

We do, as the owner. There is no landlord in the middle of this arrangement — Jai Shivmayam Trading Company Private Limited owns Office No. 425, Fourth Floor, and offers it as a shared flexi-seat facility. The NOC is issued under a board resolution of the company and signed by a director.

Most providers are tenants themselves and must ask permission from someone else. That is where delays and rejections come from.

What are my obligations under the virtual office agreement?

The agreement is notarised and specific. You undertake to file your returns and remain compliant. Physical control and lock-and-key rights over the premises stay with us. Consent runs for eleven months and twenty-nine days and is renewable.

If you default with the tax authorities or with us, the agreement terminates immediately and we exercise the authority you grant us to have your name removed from our address. We would rather tell you this now than discover it later.

Which registrations can I obtain at this address?

GST, Professional Tax (PTEC and PTRC), MSME/Udyam, Shops & Establishment, Import Export Code, company incorporation with the MCA as your registered office address under the Companies Act, and bank account opening. Each has its own document set, which we prepare.

Do you provide a telephone answering service?

Not at present. We do not want to advertise something we have not built properly. What we do provide is the physical service — a staffed office, and visitors met in person — which no competitor at this price offers at all.
