10 — Legality, answered plainly

The questions your
CA will actually ask

How much does a virtual office in Ahmedabad cost?

At IBC Ahmedabad a business address is ₹6,000 per year. A registered address suitable for GST and other government registrations is ₹8,999 per year at launch pricing, rising to ₹10,999 as standard. Managed Presence, which includes visitor receptions and meeting room hours, is ₹23,999 per year.

Registration plans additionally require a one-time ₹3,000 for statutory documentation — ₹1,400 government stamp paper, ₹600 notary and ₹1,000 advocate drafting — passed on at actuals with no margin added. All prices are exclusive of 18% GST.

Is a virtual office address legal for GST registration in India?

Yes. It is acceptable as a Principal Place of Business under the CGST Act 2017, provided the documentation requirements are met. The governing instruction is CBIC Instruction No. 03/2025-GST dated 17 April 2025, covering all premises including shared and rented space.

Can several companies register GST at the same address?

Yes, and it is settled. Section 25(2) CGST Act and Rule 11 permit it, and the Authority for Advance Ruling held in Space Lance Office Solutions Pvt Ltd that there is no restriction on multiple businesses sharing one registered office, each with its own GST registration.

What happens if a GST officer visits the virtual office address?

We are here — that is the entire point of this business. The premises run as a six-seat flexi coworking setup. Your branding goes on an available seat, your name board carries your GST number, and your file is ready at reception. Our staff support your representative through the verification. Registrations fail when nobody is present and nothing is on the wall.

Who issues the No Objection Certificate for the address?

We do, as the owner. No landlord sits in the middle — Jai Shivmayam Trading Company Private Limited owns Office No. 425 and offers it as a shared flexi-seat facility. The NOC is issued under board resolution and signed by a director.

Most providers are tenants and must ask someone else. That is where delays come from.

What are my obligations under the virtual office agreement?

Notarised and specific. You undertake to file your returns and stay compliant. Lock-and-key rights stay with us. Consent runs eleven months and twenty-nine days, renewable.

Default with the tax authorities or with us and the agreement terminates immediately, and we exercise the authority you grant us to remove your name from the address.

Which registrations can I obtain at this address?

GST, Professional Tax (PTEC and PTRC), MSME/Udyam, Shops & Establishment, Import Export Code, company incorporation with the MCA as your registered office address under the Companies Act, and bank account opening. Each has its own document set, which we prepare.

Do you provide a telephone answering service?

Not at present. What we do provide is the physical service — a staffed office, and visitors met in person — which no competitor at this price offers.
